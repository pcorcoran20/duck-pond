Irrigoto Calibration
Run Pressure → Throw first — complete it before speed cal, jog cal, or zone creation. The other calibrations use its pressure map to open the valve; until it completes they run with the valve closed (dry).
Pressure → Throw
Sweeps the valve open→closed, reads pressure at each step, then averages opening and closing readings to remove hysteresis. Water supply must be connected and pressurised. Allow several minutes for the sweep, plus the throw-distance measurement steps.

Nozzle Speed
Runs the nozzle at each PWM duty level and measures deg/sec from the encoder. CW then CCW. Takes ~4 minutes. With a completed pressure cal (and water connected) the valve opens to ~30% pressure; without one it runs dry with the valve closed.
Jog Pulse Calibration
Measures how far the nozzle moves per PWM pulse under water load. Run AFTER nozzle speed cal. With a completed pressure cal (and water connected) the valve opens to ~30% pressure; without one it runs dry with the valve closed.
